Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Little Solutions (“we”, “our”, or “us”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile applications and related services.

Summary: Our apps are designed to be simple and privacy-conscious. We do not sell your personal information. Many features work on your device, and we only collect information needed to operate, improve, protect, and support our apps.

Information We Collect

Depending on the app and the features you use, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Usage data: such as app launches, feature usage, crashes, diagnostics, device type, operating system version, and general performance information.
  • Purchase data: such as subscription status, product identifiers, purchase validation data, and renewal status handled through Apple App Store services.
  • Device permissions: certain apps may request access to features such as camera, photos, files, contacts, notifications, or local storage only when needed for the app’s functionality.
  • Support information: if you contact us, we may receive your email address and any information you choose to include in your message.

How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide and maintain app functionality.
  • Process subscriptions and in-app purchases.
  • Improve app performance, reliability, design, and user experience.
  • Detect, prevent, and fix crashes, bugs, abuse, or technical issues.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Comply with legal obligations and App Store requirements.

On-Device Data

Some app features may process content directly on your device, such as photos, videos, documents, QR codes, contacts, scans, or files. Unless a feature clearly states otherwise, this content is used locally to provide the requested functionality and is not sold by us.

Third-Party Services

We may use trusted third-party services to support analytics, crash reporting, payments, subscriptions, advertising measurement, or app functionality. These services may process limited data according to their own privacy policies. Examples may include Apple services, analytics providers, crash reporting tools, or advertising attribution platforms.

Subscriptions and Purchases

Payments and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your full payment card details. Subscription management, billing, cancellation, refunds, and renewal settings are handled by Apple.

Advertising and Analytics

Some of our apps may use analytics or advertising measurement tools to understand app performance and improve our products. Where required, we will request permission before accessing identifiers used for tracking.

Children’s Privacy

Our services are not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children where parental consent is required by applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review and remove it where appropriate.

Data Retention

We keep information only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law. Data stored locally on your device may remain there until you delete it or uninstall the app.

Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

You may be able to control data and permissions through your device settings, including access to photos, camera, contacts, notifications, local files, and tracking permissions. You can also delete the app to remove locally stored app data from your device, subject to your device and cloud backup settings.

International Users

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own. Where required, we take appropriate steps to protect information according to applicable laws.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will update the “Last updated” date above. Continued use of our apps after changes means you accept the updated policy.
